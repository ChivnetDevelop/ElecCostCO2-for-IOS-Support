# ElecCostCO2-for-IOS-Support

A simple, fast and easy-to-use calculator for estimating domestic electrical appliance energy consumption, electricity costs and associated CO2-equivalent (CO₂eq) emissions.

Explore how different appliance power ratings, operating times, electricity rates and duty cycles affect estimated energy consumption, running costs and emissions.

Features:
	•	Estimate electricity consumption, running costs and CO₂eq emissions for typical domestic electrical appliance loads.
	•	Adjustable electrical load range from 40 Watts to 10,000 Watts.
	•	Adjustable operating time from 1 to 24 hours.
	•	Adjustable electricity rate from 10 to 50 cents per kWh.(kilowatt-hour)
	•	Adjustable duty cycle from 0.25 to 1.0. (25% to 100%)
	•	Automatic recalculation whenever power rating, electricity rate, operating time or duty cycle selections are changed.
	•	Typical domestic appliance examples corresponding to selected power ratings.
	•	Standalone operation with no internet connection required.
	•	No advertising.

How to Use:
	1	Select Power Rating: Scroll through the central power selection menu and press the required wattage rating. Press the small downward arrow to expand the card and view examples of similar domestic appliances.
	2	Select Electricity Rate: Adjust and scroll through the ¢/kWh selection menu and press the required electricity rate.
	3	Select Operating Time: Adjust and scroll through the Hours selection menu and press the required operating time.
	4	Select Duty Cycle: Adjust and scroll through the Cycle selection menu and press the required duty cycle.
	5	Automatic Calculations: The app automatically recalculates energy consumption, electricity costs and emissions whenever a selection is changed. Displayed results are rounded to the nearest decimal place for calculated value.
	6	Information: Press the "?" buttons to display additional information menus.

Calculation Information and References:
Appliance examples are indicative only. Appliances corresponding to the selected wattage ratings are similar examples, and actual appliance power ratings may vary above or below the values shown.
CO₂eq emissions estimates are based on representative medium lifecycle emissions factors for coal-fired electricity generation and rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) electricity generation, derived from IPCC assessment data.
Reference: IPCC Working Group III, Fifth Assessment Report, Annex III – Technology-specific Cost and Performance Parameters.
Appliance wattage examples are based on various reference sources.
Electricity rates (cents per kWh) are based on Australian currency.

Important Information:
This application is intended for individual domestic electricity-use estimates only. It is not designed for multiple-load, large-scale, commercial or industrial applications.
All calculations are estimates based on the selected input values and reference factors. Actual electricity consumption, costs and emissions may vary depending on appliance characteristics, operating conditions, electricity tariffs and electricity generation sources.
Results should not be considered professional engineering, financial or regulatory advice. Independently verify calculations before relying on them for important decisions.
