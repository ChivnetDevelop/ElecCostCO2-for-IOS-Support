How to Use App:  
	1	Select Watts Rating: Scroll through the central Watts selection menu and press the required wattage rating. Press the small downward arrow to expand the card and view examples of similar domestic appliances.  
	2	Select Electricity Rate: Adjust and scroll through the ¢ Rate (cents/kWh) selection menu and press the required electricity rate.  
	3	Select Operating Time: Adjust and scroll through the Hours selection menu and press the required operating time.  
	4	Select Duty Cycle: Adjust and scroll through the Cycle selection menu and press the required duty cycle. Note: Duty Cycle would cover mechanisms like thermostat controls.  
	5	Automatic Calculations: The app automatically recalculates energy consumption, electricity costs and emissions whenever a selection is changed. Displayed results are rounded to the nearest decimal place for calculated value.  
	6	Information: Press the "?" buttons to display additional information menus.  

Calculation Information and References:  
Appliance examples are indicative only. Appliances corresponding to the selected wattage ratings are similar examples, and actual appliance power ratings may vary above or below the values shown.
CO₂eq emissions estimates are based on representative single source medium lifecycle emissions factors for coal-fired electricity generation or rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) electricity generation, derived from IPCC assessment data.  
Emission calculations are single source generating technologies only. A modern electricity supply grid may comprise a mixture of generating technologies (eg coal, wind and solar). This app only provides for indicative single sources only. In addition no allowance has been made for transmission losses or appliance manufacturing, transport or disposal lifecycle factors.

Reference:   IPCC Working Group III, Fifth Assessment Report, Annex III – Technology-specific Cost and Performance Parameters.  
Appliance wattage examples are based on various reference sources.  
Electricity rates (cents per kWh) are based on Australian currency.  

Important Information:  
This application is intended for individual domestic electricity-use estimates only. It is not designed for multiple-load, large-scale, commercial or industrial applications.  
All calculations are estimates based on the selected input values and reference factors. Actual electricity consumption, costs and emissions may vary depending on appliance characteristics, operating conditions, electricity tariffs and the mixture of electricity generation sources.  
Results should not be considered professional engineering, financial or regulatory advice. Independently verify calculations before relying on them for important decisions.  
In comparing with electricity bills, a number of factors may be included in standard electricity bills including daily usage charges, off peak rates, time of use, solar rebates, various applicable discounts and maximum demand and power factor charges where applicable.  
Where solar or other similar standalone sources of supply are operating in parallel to the retailer supply issuing the electricity bill, the retailer energy contribution will be less than if solely derived from the retailer's supply.
